A few thoughtful details

Your visit, made simple.

  • Choose comfortable, modest clothing that covers shoulders and knees. Ask your host about any additional prayer hall guidance.
  • Remove your shoes before entering the carpeted prayer area.
  • Keep phones silent and give worshippers space during prayers.
  • Ask permission before photographing people or prayer activities.
  • Families and curious visitors of all faiths are welcome. Bring your questions.
  • Contact your host ahead of time about step-free access or other support you need.
